Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

On May 12, 2017, the WannaCry ransomware spread across the world, infecting more than 200,000 computers in about 150 countries. The malware locked users' files and demanded a ransom payment in Bitcoin to unlock them. It spread by exploiting a weakness in older versions of Microsoft Windows that had not been updated. One of the hardest-hit organizations was the United Kingdom's National Health Service, where hospitals had to cancel appointments and turn away patients. WannaCry was significant in cybersecurity history because it showed how quickly malware can spread and why organizations must install security updates as soon as they are released.
