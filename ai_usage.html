AI in Business

Artificial intelligence (AI) is becoming an important part of how businesses operate today. Companies use AI to analyze information, improve customer service, and automate repetitive tasks. AI can also help managers make better decisions by identifying patterns in data. However, businesses must be careful about relying too much on AI because it can sometimes provide inaccurate or biased information. Employees should review AI-generated results to make sure the information is reliable. AI can improve business efficiency when it is used responsibly and with proper human oversight.

Correct AI Usage

AI can be used responsibly by students to better understand difficult concepts and improve their learning. For example, students can use AI to explain topics, brainstorm ideas, and review their work. However, students should still complete their own assignments and verify AI-generated information. It should be used as a supplementary tool for studying & tutoring and used within your professor's guidelines.

Incorrect AI Usage

Academic Dishonesty
Using AI to complete assignments without doing your own work.

Sharing Personal Information
Entering private or sensitive information into AI tools.

Overreliance on AI
Depending on AI for answers without checking if the information is accurate.
